# Bulldog Bounce

A browser game based on a home video of two English Bulldogs chasing a big blue yoga ball around the living room.

You play the bulldog. Your human sits on the couch and bounces the yoga ball into the room. Bump it before the ring under the ball runs out to score, then herd it back to the couch for the next bounce.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, on a phone or a computer. It is a single file with no build step. Fonts load from Google Fonts when you are online.

To play from a link, turn on GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch → `main` / `(root)` → Save**. After a minute or two the game will be at https://pbnjelly05.github.io/Bulldog-bounce/.

## How to play

- Pick your pup: fawn and white, or red and white. The other one naps on the dog bed.
- When your human bounces the ball, a ring appears under it. Every bump before the ring runs out scores.
- Herd the ball back to the couch to get the next bounce.
- A round lasts 90 seconds.

About 30 seconds in, the napping pup wakes up and chases bounces too. If it bumps the ball first, that bounce is stolen and your combo ends.

### Controls

| Action | Phone or tablet | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | Drag anywhere | Arrow keys or WASD |
| Lunge | Dash button | Space |
| Pause | Pause button | P or Esc |

### Scoring

| Event | Points |
| --- | --- |
| First bump after a bounce | 100 |
| Each extra bump on the same bounce | 50 more than the last, up to 300 |
| Bump while the ball is in the air | 50 bonus |
| Herd the ball back to your human | 50 |
| Grab a chew bone (5 seconds of zoomies) | 25 |

Your best score is saved in the browser you play in.
